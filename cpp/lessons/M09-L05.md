## ⚠️ Best Practices & Common Pitfalls
- **Gunakan RAII**: Selalu kelola resource (memori, file, mutex) menggunakan objek berbasis scope (seperti `std::unique_ptr`, `std::lock_guard`).
- **Hindari Pointer Mentah (Raw Owning Pointer)**: Jangan gunakan `new` dan `delete` manual di C++ modern.
- **Waspada Undefined Behavior (UB)**: Hindari dangling references, out-of-bounds array access, dan uninitialized variables.
